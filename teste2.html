Foundations from pandas Time Series

Before visual diagnostics, define what time means

Timestamp → calendar → frequency → alignment → resampling → windows → graphics

Este bloco deve ser inserido antes da seção Data and Time Structure da aula principal. A ideia é preparar a infraestrutura conceitual necessária para interpretar time plots, seasonal plots, lag plots e ACF.

[Sources] - https://wesmckinney.com/book/time-series

What can a time coordinate represent?

  • Timestamp: a specific instant.
  • Fixed period: a span such as a month or quarter.
  • Interval: a start and an end.
  • Elapsed time: distance from an origin.

The representation must match what the observation actually measures.

Use preço de fechamento como exemplo de timestamp e produção mensal como período. Explique que a escolha altera slicing, agregação e labels.

[Sources] - https://wesmckinney.com/book/time-series

Fixed-frequency and irregular series

Fixed frequency

  • every 15 seconds;
  • hourly;
  • business daily;
  • monthly.

Irregular

  • trades;
  • incidents;
  • publications;
  • event records.

A line can display either, but lag 1 has a stable duration only under a regular calendar.

Em série irregular, shift(1) significa registro anterior, não necessariamente uma hora anterior.

UTC and local calendar features

UTC supports consistent storage and cross-region alignment. Local time remains important for:

  • hour-of-day behavior;
  • market sessions;
  • business operations;
  • daylight-saving transitions;
  • calendar seasonality.

Derive seasonal coordinates in the calendar relevant to the phenomenon.

A recomendação é separar alinhamento temporal de interpretação sazonal.

Resampling changes frequency

hourly = pd.Series(
    np.arange(24, dtype=float),
    index=pd.date_range("2026-01-01", periods=24, freq="h")
)
hourly.resample("6h").agg(["mean", "max"])
mean max
2026-01-01 00:00:00 2.5 5.0
2026-01-01 06:00:00 8.5 11.0
2026-01-01 12:00:00 14.5 17.0
2026-01-01 18:00:00 20.5 23.0
  • Downsampling: higher to lower frequency, usually with aggregation.
  • Upsampling: lower to higher frequency, usually creating gaps before a filling rule.

Mean e max respondem perguntas diferentes. A função de agregação pertence à definição do gráfico.

Moving windows summarize local history

observed = pd.Series(
    [10, 13, 12, 16, 17, 15, 18],
    index=pd.date_range("2026-01-01", periods=7, freq="D")
)
windows = pd.DataFrame({
    "observed": observed,
    "rolling_mean_3": observed.rolling(3, min_periods=2).mean(),
    "expanding_mean": observed.expanding(min_periods=2).mean(),
    "ewm_mean": observed.ewm(span=3, adjust=False).mean()
})
