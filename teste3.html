Time Series Graphics

Why start with graphs?

Graphs help reveal:

  • patterns and changes through time;
  • unusual observations;
  • seasonal behavior;
  • relationships between variables;
  • temporal dependence.

Visual features should inform the forecasting method considered later.

Este material acompanha a sequência e os exemplos gráficos do Capítulo 2, com redação própria. Os exemplos utilizam os arquivos CSV locais informados pelo usuário.

Time-plot reading protocol

Look for:

  1. level and long-run direction;
  2. fixed-period repetition;
  3. changes in variability;
  4. unusual observations and gaps;
  5. structural breaks or interventions;
  6. whether the visible patterns are stable.

Descrição visual vem antes de explicação causal.

2.3 Time series patterns

Trend, seasonal, and cyclic behavior

  • Trend: long-term increase or decrease; it need not be linear.
  • Seasonal: repetition with a fixed, known, calendar-related period.
  • Cyclic: rises and falls without a fixed frequency.

A forecasting method should be able to represent the patterns visible in the data.

O principal contraste é fixed known period versus duração variável.

[Sources] - https://otexts.com/fpppy/02-graphics.html

Correlation measures linear association

r = \frac{\sum_t (x_t-\bar{x})(y_t-\bar{y})}
{\sqrt{\sum_t(x_t-\bar{x})^2}\sqrt{\sum_t(y_t-\bar{y})^2}}
  • r lies between -1 and 1;
  • sign indicates direction;
  • magnitude describes linear association;
  • nonlinear structure can be strong even when r is modest.

Use o scatterplot anterior para mostrar por que um coeficiente único pode ser insuficiente.

[Sources] - https://otexts.com/fpppy/02-graphics.html

2.8 Autocorrelation

Definition

r_k = \frac{\sum_{t=k+1}^{T}(y_t-\bar{y})(y_{t-k}-\bar{y})}
{\sum_{t=1}^{T}(y_t-\bar{y})^2}

Autocorrelation measures linear association between a series and its lagged values. The collection of coefficients is the ACF.

Cada coeficiente resume um dos painéis do lag plot.

[Sources] - https://otexts.com/fpppy/02-graphics.html

Trend and seasonality in ACF plots

  • trend often produces large positive autocorrelations that decay slowly;
  • seasonality produces peaks at seasonal lags;
  • trend plus seasonality combines slow decay with repeated seasonal structure.

A ACF não substitui o time plot; os dois gráficos respondem perguntas complementares.

Key takeaways

Visual diagnostic sequence

  1. validate time, identifiers, and frequency;
  2. begin with a time plot;
  3. identify trend, seasonality, and cycles;
  4. reorganize time with seasonal and subseries plots;
  5. inspect relationships with scatterplots;
  6. inspect serial dependence with lag plots and ACF;
  7. compare residual-looking behavior with white noise;
  8. convert visual findings into forecasting requirements.

O gráfico é parte do raciocínio de forecasting, não apenas uma etapa de comunicação.
