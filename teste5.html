Use para ensinar comunicação de incerteza descritiva. Dados sintéticos e amostra curta limitam interpretação. Tempo: 3 min.

Titles, labels, annotations, and legends

A complete chart should answer:

  • What is being shown?
  • Over which population or sample?
  • In which units?
  • Which visual channels encode variables?
  • What feature deserves attention?
  • What limitation constrains interpretation?

Títulos devem comunicar a mensagem, mas não exagerar a evidência. Tempo: 2 min.

Accessibility and restraint

  • use sufficient contrast;
  • avoid relying only on color;
  • label axes with units;
  • remove redundant legends;
  • use direct and informative titles;
  • annotate only relevant evidence;
  • avoid 3D effects and decorative distortion.

Acessibilidade melhora precisão e reduz ambiguidade. Tempo: 2 min.

Checkpoint 4

Choose and justify a chart for:

  1. evolution of one instrument;
  2. distribution of returns by instrument;
  3. relationship between volume and return;
  4. weekly standardized values;
  5. proportions by category.

State the unit and one interpretive limitation.

Possíveis respostas: linha, box/violin, scatter, heatmap, barras proporcionais. Avalie coerência entre pergunta, resumo e gráfico. Tempo: 4 min.

Choose the visual channel deliberately

Data property Strong visual channel
quantitative magnitude aligned position
ordered time horizontal position and connection
category position, color, or shape
distribution position plus density or intervals
uncertainty intervals, bands, or distributions

Use color to support structure, not to compensate for weak comparisons.

Apresente como orientação baseada em percepção, não regra universal. Tempo: 3 min.

Line plots require meaningful order

brent_line = chart_ready.loc[chart_ready["instrument"].eq("BRENT")].sort_values("date")
fig, ax = plt.subplots()
ax.plot(brent_line["date"], brent_line["price"], marker="o", color="#0072B2")
ax.set(title="A line encodes ordered continuity",
       xlabel="Date", ylabel="Price (USD/bbl)")
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

Linha conecta observações e implica ordem. Não use linha para categorias nominais sem ordem. Tempo: 3 min.

Bars need a meaningful baseline

crude_summary = crude.groupby("instrument", as_index=False).agg(
    mean_price=("price", "mean")
)
fig, ax = plt.subplots()
sns.barplot(data=crude_summary, x="instrument", y="mean_price", color="#4472C4", ax=ax)
ax.set_ylim(0, None)
ax.set(title="Mean crude-oil price", xlabel="Instrument", ylabel="Mean price (USD/bbl)")
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

Because bar length encodes magnitude, a zero baseline is normally required.

Compare com dot plot quando diferenças pequenas são o foco. Tempo: 3 min.

Histograms depend on bin choices

fig, axes = plt.subplots(1, 2, sharey=True)
axes[0].hist(chart_ready["return_pct"].dropna(), bins=4, color="#4472C4")
axes[1].hist(chart_ready["return_pct"].dropna(), bins=10, color="#4472C4")
axes[0].set(title="4 bins", xlabel="Return (%)", ylabel="Frequency")
axes[1].set(title="10 bins", xlabel="Return (%)")
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

The apparent shape changes with bin width and sample size.

Ensine sensibilidade a bins. Para amostras pequenas, ECDF ou pontos individuais podem ser mais transparentes. Tempo: 3 min.

Density and ECDF answer different questions

fig, axes = plt.subplots(1, 2)
sns.kdeplot(data=chart_ready, x="price_z", hue="instrument", common_norm=False, ax=axes[0])
sns.ecdfplot(data=chart_ready, x="price_z", hue="instrument", ax=axes[1])
axes[0].set(title="Smoothed density", xlabel="Price z-score", ylabel="Density")
axes[1].set(title="Empirical cumulative distribution", xlabel="Price z-score", ylabel="Cumulative share")
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

KDE depende de bandwidth; ECDF preserva todos os pontos sem bins ou smoothing. Tempo: 4 min.

Faceting prevents incompatible overlays

facet = sns.relplot(
    data=chart_ready, x="date", y="price",
    col="price_unit", hue="instrument", kind="line",
    marker="o", facet_kws={"sharey": False}, height=4, aspect=1.15
)
facet.set_axis_labels("Date", "Price in stated unit")
facet.figure.suptitle("Separate panels preserve unit context", y=1.05)
plt.show()

Facets separam unidades, mas sharey=False dificulta comparar magnitude vertical entre painéis. Declare essa limitação. Tempo: 3 min.

Regression lines are models, not decorations

plot_reg = chart_ready.dropna(subset=["volume", "return_pct"])
fig, ax = plt.subplots()
sns.regplot(data=plot_reg, x="volume", y="return_pct",
            scatter_kws={"alpha": 0.7}, line_kws={"color": "#D55E00"}, ax=ax)
ax.set(title="A fitted line summarizes an assumed relationship",
       xlabel="Volume", ylabel="Daily return (%)")
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

A regression line does not establish causality and may conceal group structure.

Destaque amostra pequena, agrupamento ignorado e causalidade. Tempo: 3 min.

Legends, direct labels, and redundant encodings

A legend is useful when:

  • several series share one plotting area;
  • labels would otherwise overlap;
  • color or style has a clear semantic mapping.

Prefer direct labels when a small number of series can be identified at their endpoints. Combine color and marker shape when accessibility requires redundancy.

Discuta custo cognitivo de mover os olhos entre linhas e legenda. Tempo: 2 min.

Export vector and raster formats intentionally

fig, ax = plt.subplots(figsize=(8, 4.5))
ax.plot(brent_line["date"], brent_line["price"], color="#0072B2")
ax.set(title="Exportable analytical figure", xlabel="Date", ylabel="Price (USD/bbl)")
fig.tight_layout()
fig.savefig("analytical_figure.png", dpi=160, bbox_inches="tight")
fig.savefig("analytical_figure.svg", bbox_inches="tight")
plt.close(fig)

PNG is raster-based; SVG is vector-based and scales cleanly for many publication uses.

Explique escolha de formato de saída e reprodução local. Tempo: 2 min.

Integrated Case

From analysis-ready data to visual evidence

Question:

How did average daily movement vary by instrument and week in the synthetic sample?

Requirements:

  • compute returns within instrument;
  • aggregate by instrument and week;
  • retain coverage and dispersion;
  • validate group keys and denominators;
  • visualize on a common percentage scale.

Este caso integra toda a segunda aula. Tempo: 2 min.

Aggregate with context

weekly = (
    chart_ready.groupby(["instrument", "week"], as_index=False)
    .agg(
        mean_return=("return_pct", "mean"),
        return_sd=("return_pct", "std"),
        observed_returns=("return_pct", "count"),
        total_volume=("volume", "sum")
    )
)
weekly = weekly.dropna(subset=["mean_return"]).reset_index(drop=True)
weekly.round(2)
instrument week mean_return return_sd observed_returns total_volume
0 BRENT 1 762.08 1076.63 2 403.0
1 TTF 1 4.15 <NA> 1 175.0
2 WTI 1 0.9 0.01 2 203.0

A média é acompanhada por cobertura, dispersão e volume. Tempo: 2 min.

Validate the analytical table

assert weekly[["instrument", "week"]].duplicated().sum() == 0
assert weekly["observed_returns"].ge(0).all()
assert weekly["observed_returns"].le(7).all()
assert weekly["mean_return"].notna().all()

As invariantes verificam chave única e coerência com a semana. Tempo: 2 min.

Communicate the result

fig, ax = plt.subplots()
sns.lineplot(data=weekly, x="week", y="mean_return",
             hue="instrument", marker="o", ax=ax)
ax.axhline(0, color="0.35", linewidth=1)
ax.set(title="Weekly mean daily return in synthetic data",
       xlabel="Week", ylabel="Mean daily return (%)")
ax.legend(title="Instrument", frameon=False)
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

Interprete apenas a amostra sintética. A escala percentual melhora comparabilidade de movimento, não equivalência econômica. Tempo: 3 min.

Final Practice

Capstone exercise

Using raw and metadata:

  1. define the observational unit;
  2. inspect types and labels;
  3. diagnose and clean quality problems;
  4. preserve an audit trail;
  5. perform a validated merge;
  6. reshape if required;
  7. formulate a grouped question;
  8. aggregate and retain denominators;
  9. validate at least five assumptions;
  10. create and interpret one defensible visualization.

Avalie processo, justificativas e testes, não apenas o resultado visual. Tempo sugerido: atividade integradora da segunda aula.

Suggested assessment rubric

Dimension Evidence
Structure observational unit and dtypes are explicit
Quality problems and treatments are documented
Relationships keys and cardinality are validated
Analysis grouping and denominator answer the question
Visualization encoding, units, and labels are appropriate
Interpretation claims remain within the evidence
Reproducibility code executes and assertions pass

Use a rubrica para feedback formativo e avaliação do projeto. Tempo: 3 min.
